Sand plant equipment

Equipment for washing, dewatering and classifying sand, and for treating the muddy water.

  • Sand Unit

    Recovers and dewaters washed sand. A sand pump feeds a cyclone that removes fines below 0.1–0.15 mm, and a dewatering screen removes the water.

    Request a quote for Sand Unit

  • Thickener

    Treats the muddy water from the sand unit with chemicals so that the fines settle out. A slow-turning scraper concentrates the sludge, and clear water overflows to the clear-water tank.

    Request a quote for Thickener

  • Filter Press

    Presses sludge into filter cake under high pressure (feed pressure 2–20 kgf/cm², about 2–20 bar). Filtering, pressing, cake discharge and cloth washing can run automatically.

    Request a quote for Filter Press

  • Sand Classifier

    Washes, classifies and dewaters sand in one unit — classifier, dewatering screen and cyclone. Recovers fine sand.

    Request a quote for Sand Classifier

Company

About Dae Suk Industrial

Dae Suk Industrial Co., Ltd. is a manufacturer based in Seongju, Gyeongsangbuk-do, Korea, founded in 2012. We make crushing and screening equipment for aggregate production: jaw, cone and vertical shaft impact crushers, vibrating and scalping screens, and vibrating and apron feeders.

We also fabricate and install crushing plants and construction waste treatment plants, and maintain industrial machinery. Drawing on our experience in plant design and on-site management, we work toward automated plant operation, fast execution and close control of every production stage.
