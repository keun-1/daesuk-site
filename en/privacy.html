Privacy policy

This is an English translation for convenience. If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

DAE SUK INDUSTRIAL CO., LTD. (the “Company”) has established and publishes this privacy policy under the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, to protect the personal information of data subjects and to handle related complaints smoothly.

1. Purpose

We process personal information to reply to quotation and consultation inquiries received through the website inquiry form, text message, email or telephone.

2. Items processed

(Required) Name of the contact person and phone number. (Optional) Company name, email address and inquiry details. These are the items that the data subject enters in the inquiry form or gives us by text message, email or telephone.

3. Processing and retention period

We destroy personal information without delay once the inquiry has been handled. If an inquiry leads to a contract, the related transaction records are kept under Article 33 of the Commercial Act (Preservation of Commercial Books, etc.): important business documents for 10 years, and slips and similar documents for 5 years.

4. Destruction procedure and method

When the retention period ends or the purpose has been achieved, we destroy the information without delay. Electronic files are deleted so that they cannot be restored, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

5. Provision to third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties, except with the consent of the data subject or where the law allows it.

6. Outsourcing

We receive inquiries through the Company email account (hanmail.net) and text messages to the Company mobile phone. We do not outsource any other processing of personal information.

7. Rights of data subjects and how to exercise them

Data subjects may ask to access, correct or delete their personal information, or to suspend its processing. Contact us as shown below and we will act without delay.

8. Security measures

We keep the number of people who handle personal information to a minimum, protect the email account and mobile phone used for inquiries with passwords or other locks, and keep printed documents in a locked place.

9. Automatic collection devices

This website does not itself install or operate cookies or similar devices that collect personal information automatically. However, external services included in the pages, such as Google Maps, are subject to the policies of their providers.

11. Remedies

For advice on or reports of privacy infringement, you may contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (118 within Korea, privacy.kisa.or.kr), the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972 within Korea, www.kopico.go.kr), the Supreme Prosecutors' Office (1301 within Korea, www.spo.go.kr) or the Korean National Police Agency (182 within Korea).

12. Effective date

This policy is effective from 1 November 2026.
